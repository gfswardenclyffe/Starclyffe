The story

Every city once had a garden on its roof

"Follow the light, and see what you discover."

Above the cities of Starclyffe hangs a sun made of lights. Each light is a world, and each world keeps a garden. When the Fungi Kingdom began to spread, the gardens went grey one by one, and the sun grew dim.

Elian Chrysanthos wears a ceremonial crown and carries a rapier that is also a sword lily. Where it strikes, spores turn to petals and the roofs remember what grew there.

His first city is New York. Each rooftop he heals relights a small part of the sun. Paris is next.

Characters

Who you will meet on the rooftops

Elian Chrysanthos

The Flower Prince

A quiet boy with dense black curls, a Benkan crown and a cape far too big for him. His sword lily turns rot into petals.

The Mushroom Witch

Servant of the Fungi Kingdom

She walks on stilts and watches from under her cap with one eye. When her bells ring, spores rise from the mushrooms on her hat.

Spore bugs

The Fungi Kingdom's swarm

Round black beetles that drift over the streets and sow violet spores on every roof they pass.
